Risk & Compliance

Compliance Frameworks Built Into the Servicing Layer

Rather than treating compliance as a separate function, our servicing infrastructure is built around the operating guidelines that govern ACH disbursement and commercial debt enforcement from day one.

Security & Data Governance

Common Questions

Risk & Compliance FAQ

Is Veteran Loan Servicing NACHA certified?

Veteran Loan Servicing structures its ACH payment processing around NACHA operating guidelines. NACHA does not certify individual companies; rather, it publishes the operating rules that govern ACH transactions in the United States.

What happens if a borrower defaults?

Defaulted accounts move into a structured debt management process, with remedies pursued under UCC Article 9 where applicable to the secured commercial asset.
