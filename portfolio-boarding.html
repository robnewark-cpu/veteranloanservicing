Servicing Operations

Portfolio boarding, reconciled before go-live

Boarding is where servicing risk is won or lost. We migrate loan data, payment schedules, and lender disbursement details into the platform and reconcile them before a single payment cycle runs.

Process

Boarding runs as a defined sequence: portfolio review, data intake, mapping into the servicing ledger, reconciliation against your source of record, and a go-live checkpoint. Nothing goes live until balances, rates, and schedules tie out.

  • Portfolio review and scope confirmation
  • Loan, borrower, and lender data intake
  • Mapping into the servicing ledger
  • Reconciliation against your source of record
  • Go-live checkpoint and sign-off

Controls

Each boarded record is checked against the source file. Discrepancies are flagged and resolved before go-live rather than discovered mid-cycle. The boarding trail is retained so the migration itself is auditable.

Reporting

You receive a reconciliation summary at go-live: loans boarded, balances tied out, and any items held for clarification. From cycle one, boarded loans appear in standard portfolio reporting.

Benefits

A clean boarding reduces the disputes, misapplied payments, and reporting gaps that follow a rushed migration — and gives lenders and LPs confidence that the book was moved correctly.

FAQ

What data do you need to board a portfolio?

Loan-level balances, rates, payment schedules, borrower contact details, and lender disbursement instructions. We confirm the exact intake format during portfolio review.

How are boarded records verified?

Every boarded record is reconciled against your source of record before go-live; discrepancies are resolved first, not after payments begin.

Do you board residential loans?

No. Veteran Loan Servicing boards commercial real estate and business/commercial loans only. We do not service consumer residential mortgages.
