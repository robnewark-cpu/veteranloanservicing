Regulatory Notice & Scope of Operations: Veteran Loan Servicing operates strictly as an institutional B2B enterprise portfolio sub-servicing platform. Our solution layers are engineered exclusively for corporate alternative credit configurations, commercial real estate assets, business/commercial loan portfolios, and industrial insurance compliance tracking. This platform does not maintain licensing configurations for, nor does it conduct transactions involving, consumer residential mortgages, retail family banking products, or public consumer lending systems subject to localized residential mortgage licensing acts.
