CRE insurance compliance monitoring inside servicing

Expired certificates are a quiet portfolio risk. When coverage tracking lives in broker portals and inboxes, lapses surface too late — at claim time or covenant review.

The compliance gap

Servicing teams can remittance-perfect a book and still miss that a policy lapsed last month. Insurance oversight belongs in the same operating layer as payments and delinquency — not a side spreadsheet.

How we structure monitoring

Required coverage inventory per asset, ongoing tracking of certificates, automated alerts when a policy approaches lapse or falls out of compliance, and escalation paths before the collateral is unprotected.

  • Portfolio-level view of coverage status
  • Alerts tied to servicing workflows
  • Reporting suitable for credit and risk committees

Built for institutional CRE

Designed for commercial real estate and business/commercial loan portfolios — not consumer home insurance products.

FAQ

Is insurance monitoring separate from servicing?

No. We treat corporate insurance compliance as part of the servicing operating matrix so gaps are visible alongside payment and delinquency status.

Who receives lapse alerts?

Escalation is configured with your credit/ops team during onboarding so the right owners are notified before coverage fails.
