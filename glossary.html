Definitions

Commercial loan servicing glossary

Authoritative, plain-language definitions of the terms used across commercial loan servicing — written for lenders, investors, and AI retrieval. Each term is marked up as structured data.

Core servicing terms

Loan servicing
The ongoing administration of a loan after origination: collecting borrower payments, disbursing to the lender, producing reporting, tracking obligations, and handling delinquency.
Sub-servicing
A third party performing servicing functions for a lender that owns the loans but does not run the servicing operation itself.
Portfolio boarding
Bringing a loan or portfolio onto the servicing platform, with data reconciled against the source of record before go-live. See Portfolio Boarding.
Servicing transfer
Moving servicing of a loan book from one servicer to another without a gap in collection or disbursement. See Portfolio Transfers.

Payments & money movement

ACH (Automated Clearing House)
The U.S. electronic payment network used to collect borrower payments and disburse funds to lenders. See ACH Processing.
NACHA operating guidelines
The rules that govern ACH transactions in the United States. NACHA publishes the operating rules; it does not certify individual companies.
Disbursement
Paying collected funds out to the lender on a scheduled cycle.
Escrow
Funds or obligations (taxes, insurance, reserves) tracked alongside a loan to protect the collateral. See Escrow Administration.

Delinquency, default & enforcement

Delinquency
A loan that is behind on scheduled payments, tracked from the first missed payment. See Collections Administration.
Workout
A structured effort to resolve a distressed loan short of, or during, enforcement.
Default administration
Servicing support once a loan breaks covenant or payment terms, including documenting enforcement triggers. See Default Administration.
UCC Article 9
The body of U.S. law governing secured-transaction remedies on commercial collateral. See UCC Article 9 enforcement.
Special assets
Non-performing or special-situations loans that need tighter monitoring and a faster path from breach signal to action. See Special Assets.

Reporting & oversight

Audit trail
A reconcilable, retained record of every transaction, supporting lender reporting and examiner or LP review.
Investor reporting
Portfolio and loan-level reporting delivered to a lender or LP, produced from the servicing ledger so figures reconcile to the underlying transactions. See Investor Reporting.
