UCC Article 9 enforcement starts in the servicing layer

Remedies only help if breach is noticed early and the trail is clean. For special-situations and CRE books, timing is the product — not the lawsuit after the fact.

Default is a process problem first

When delinquency and covenant signals live in quarterly packs, workout counsel arrives late. Wiring enforcement triggers into servicing shortens the gap between missed payment and structured remediation.

What we operationalize

Delinquency tracking from first miss through structured recovery workflows, documentation suitable for counsel, and Article 9-aware escalation where applicable on commercial (non-consumer) collateral structures.

  • Early signal → remediation protocol
  • Audit-ready event history
  • Coordination path for workout counsel

Scope boundaries

We operate as an institutional B2B servicing platform for commercial real estate and business/commercial loans. We do not service consumer residential mortgages or retail lending products.

FAQ

Do you provide legal advice?

No. We operate servicing and enforcement-trigger workflows. Legal strategy remains with your counsel; we provide the operational trail and escalation timing.

Is this only for non-performing loans?

Performing books benefit from early detection. Special-situations and NPL portfolios benefit most from tight breach-to-action timing.
