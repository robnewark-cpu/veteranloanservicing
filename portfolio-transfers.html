Servicing Operations

Servicing transfers without dropped payments

Whether you are moving a book off a prior servicer or acquiring a loan portfolio, the transfer is the moment borrowers and lenders judge the servicer. We plan it so collection and disbursement continue without a gap.

Process

A transfer runs as a scheduled cutover: intake from the prior servicer or seller, validation of balances and payment history, borrower and lender notification, and a defined transfer date after which collection and disbursement run on our platform.

  • Intake from prior servicer or loan seller
  • Validation of balances and payment history
  • Borrower and lender notification of the transfer
  • Defined transfer/cutover date
  • Continuity of ACH collection and lender disbursement

Controls

We validate the transferred data before the cutover date and keep a documented transfer trail. Payment instructions are confirmed so that the first post-transfer cycle collects and disburses correctly.

Reporting

You receive a transfer confirmation showing loans transferred, balances validated, and the effective transfer date. Post-transfer, the book appears in standard portfolio and disbursement reporting.

Benefits

A planned transfer protects the borrower relationship and the lender's cash flow, and gives a loan purchaser a clean, documented handover rather than an inherited mess.

FAQ

Can you transfer in a book we just purchased?

Yes. We handle transfer-in from a prior servicer or directly from a loan seller, validating data before the cutover date.

Will borrowers experience a payment gap during transfer?

The transfer is planned so ACH collection and lender disbursement continue on schedule; borrowers and lenders are notified of the effective transfer date.

How are borrower payments handled after transfer?

Borrower payments are collected via ACH and disbursed to lenders on a scheduled monthly cycle, structured around NACHA operating guidelines, with a full audit trail maintained for every transaction.
