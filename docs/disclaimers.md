# Reusable Disclaimer System

Standard disclaimer blocks. Paste the exact text into pages where the corresponding
condition applies. Rendered visually with the `.footer-notice` class or a small muted `<p>`.

## 1. Informational Purposes
> The information on this page is provided for general informational purposes about Veteran
> Loan Servicing's operational capabilities and does not constitute legal, financial, tax,
> or investment advice.

## 2. No Guarantee
> Descriptions of servicing processes reflect how Veteran Loan Servicing operates and do not
> guarantee any specific outcome, recovery, timeline, or result for any portfolio.

## 3. Future Feature / Roadmap
> Items described as planned, in progress, or on our roadmap are not current capabilities and
> may change. Only capabilities described as active or current are operational today.

## 4. Operational Capability (use where a capability is asserted as live)
> ACH payment processing is an active, current operational capability. Other capabilities are
> described as active only where explicitly stated; roadmap items are labeled as such.

## 5. Availability
> Availability of services is subject to engagement scope, portfolio eligibility (commercial
> real estate and business/commercial loans only), and a completed onboarding review. Veteran
> Loan Servicing does not service consumer residential mortgages or retail consumer lending products.

## 6. Trust Center posture (use on Trust/Security pages)
> This Trust Center describes Veteran Loan Servicing's operational practices and controls. It
> does not assert that Veteran Loan Servicing holds any specific third-party certification unless
> that certification is explicitly named and dated. Certifications listed as planned are roadmap items.
