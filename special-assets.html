Servicing Operations

Special assets — where timing is the product

For non-performing and special-situations commercial paper, the difference between recovery and loss is timing. We treat special assets as a servicing discipline with a documented trail, not an afterthought.

Process

Special-situations and non-performing loans get tighter monitoring: breach signals are surfaced early, remediation steps are tracked, and UCC Article 9 enforcement triggers are documented so action is timely.

  • Early breach detection on watch-list loans
  • Tracked remediation steps
  • Documented UCC Article 9 enforcement triggers
  • Coordination with the lender's workout counsel

Controls

Breach-to-action timing is driven by the operating trail rather than a quarterly report, and each step is documented so the special-asset file holds up.

Reporting

Watch-list and special-asset status appear in portfolio reporting, and the documented trail supports the lender's counsel and LP or examiner review.

Benefits

Performing books benefit from early detection; special-situations and NPL portfolios benefit most from tight breach-to-action timing and a clean, documented trail.

FAQ

What counts as a special asset?

Non-performing or special-situations commercial loans that need tighter monitoring and a faster path from breach signal to remediation.

Do you provide legal representation?

No. We document enforcement triggers and coordinate timing; legal remedies are pursued by the lender's own counsel.

Which portfolios benefit most?

Performing books benefit from early detection; special-situations and NPL portfolios benefit most from tight breach-to-action timing.
