Frequently Asked

Institutional Servicing FAQ

What is included in your commercial loan servicing solutions?

Our commercial loan servicing solutions include payment processing, escrow and insurance tracking, default management, investor reporting, and multi-currency reconciliation for non-residential and student loan portfolios.

Do you service consumer residential mortgages?

No. Veteran Loan Servicing operates strictly as a B2B institutional platform for non-residential commercial assets and structured student loan portfolios. We do not service consumer residential mortgages.

Can your platform onboard an existing portfolio mid-cycle?

Yes. Our onboarding framework is designed for boarding active portfolios with minimal disruption, including historical ledger migration, escrow reconciliation, and borrower notice sequencing.
