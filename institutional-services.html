Institutional Services

Commercial Real Estate & Business Loan Servicing, Under One Roof

A unified servicing, debt management, and insurance compliance layer built specifically for institutional lenders holding commercial real estate and business/commercial loan portfolios.

What We Service

Core Institutional Offerings

Every engagement is built around the same operating principle: eliminate the handoffs between servicing, debt recovery, and insurance compliance that create blind spots during default events.

Commercial Real Estate Loan Servicing

Full lifecycle servicing for commercial real estate loans, including statement cycles, escrow tracking, and disbursement processing built on NACHA-aligned ACH infrastructure.

Business & Commercial Loan Servicing

Sub-servicing for business and commercial loan positions, with automated payment collection and lender disbursement handled under one operational roof.

Enterprise Debt Management & Recovery

Structured default tracking and recovery strategy for non-performing accounts, with remedies pursued under established UCC Article 9 frameworks where applicable.

Corporate Insurance Compliance Monitoring

Ongoing tracking of required insurance coverage across serviced assets, with automated alerts when a policy lapses or falls out of compliance.

Common Questions

Institutional Servicing FAQ

What types of loans do you service?

We service commercial real estate loans and business/commercial loans exclusively. We do not service consumer residential mortgages or retail consumer lending products.

How does disbursement to lenders work?

Borrower payments are collected via ACH and disbursed to lenders on a scheduled monthly cycle, with full audit trails maintained for every transaction.

Do you handle delinquent accounts?

Yes. Our debt management process tracks accounts from first missed payment through structured recovery, including remedies available under UCC Article 9 where applicable.
