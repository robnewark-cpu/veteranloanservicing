Servicing Operations

Default administration support, documented

When a commercial loan defaults, timing and documentation decide recovery. We track the workout and surface UCC Article 9 enforcement triggers as servicing events — not as a lawsuit discovered after the fact.

Process

Accounts that break covenant or payment terms move into a delinquency-to-remediation workflow, with UCC Article 9 enforcement triggers documented in the operating trail so workout counsel is engaged on time rather than late.

Note: Veteran Loan Servicing provides default administration support as a servicer. It does not provide legal representation; legal remedies are pursued by the lender's counsel.

  • Delinquency-to-remediation workflow
  • UCC Article 9 enforcement triggers documented
  • Timely engagement of the lender's workout counsel
  • Operating trail retained for the file

Controls

Enforcement triggers live in the operating trail rather than a quarterly report, and each step is documented so the default file is defensible.

Reporting

Default and workout status appear in portfolio reporting, and the documented trail supports the lender's counsel and any examiner or LP review.

Benefits

Surfacing breach signals as servicing events — with a clean trail — protects the lender's remedies and shortens the path from default to action.

FAQ

Do you provide legal representation on defaults?

No. Veteran Loan Servicing provides default administration support as a servicer and documents enforcement triggers; legal remedies are pursued by the lender's own counsel.

How does UCC Article 9 fit in?

Where applicable, UCC Article 9 enforcement triggers are documented in the operating trail so remediation is timely and defensible.

When does an account enter default administration?

When it breaks covenant or payment terms and moves out of ordinary collections into the remediation workflow.
