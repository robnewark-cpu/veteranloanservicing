Trust Center

Built for Procurement Review

This Trust Center describes how Veteran Loan Servicing protects data, controls access, and runs its operation — written so a lender's procurement or vendor-review team can answer most questions here before a call.

We describe practices and controls we actually operate. Where a formal third-party certification is not yet held, we label it as a roadmap item rather than claim it.

What We Control

Operational & security controls

Each area below reflects how we operate today. Expanded detail pages are being published area by area.

Security Overview

Traffic runs on Cloudflare's edge network with encrypted transport (TLS). Application and site infrastructure are deployed through controlled, version-tracked releases.

Data Protection

Portfolio and borrower data are handled within the United States, encrypted in transit, and retained only as long as needed for servicing and recordkeeping obligations.

Access Controls

Access to portfolio data is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, with credentials managed and reviewed rather than shared.

Business Continuity

Servicing records and disbursement data are maintained with recovery in mind so that portfolio operations can continue through disruption. Detailed continuity documentation is available on request.

Vendor Management

Third-party providers used in servicing (payment rails, hosting) are limited and evaluated for their own security posture. We keep the vendor surface small by design.

Record Retention

Every transaction is recorded in a reconcilable audit trail, retained to meet servicing and recordkeeping obligations and support lender reporting and review.

Compliance Program

ACH activity is structured around NACHA operating guidelines; enforcement follows UCC Article 9 frameworks where applicable. Our founder is a compliance practitioner who designs controls to the intent of SOC 2, HIPAA, and ISO 27001.

Operational Controls

Collection, reconciliation, and disbursement follow a defined, documented sequence with an audit trail at each step — the same discipline applied to delinquency and insurance-compliance workflows.
