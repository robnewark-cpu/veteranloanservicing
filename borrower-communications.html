Servicing Operations

Borrower communications, documented and consistent

Borrowers are the counterparties who keep a loan performing. Clear, consistent, documented communication reduces disputes and preserves the lender relationship.

Process

Borrowers receive statement cycles and payment-related notices on a regular schedule. Servicing contact is handled by our team and recorded, so a lender can see what was communicated and when.

  • Scheduled statement cycles
  • Payment and delinquency notices
  • Documented servicing contact
  • Escalation into workout when terms are broken

Controls

Communications tied to a loan are recorded in the servicing trail. Delinquency notices follow a defined sequence rather than ad-hoc outreach.

Reporting

The communication trail is available to lenders on request and supports the documentation examiners and LPs expect around delinquency handling.

Benefits

Documented, consistent borrower contact shortens the distance between a missed payment and a resolution — and removes the 'what did the servicer actually say' ambiguity.

FAQ

Who contacts borrowers?

Our servicing team handles borrower contact directly, with the interaction recorded in the servicing trail — not routed through an anonymous ticket queue.

Are borrower communications documented?

Yes. Communications tied to a loan are recorded so a lender can see what was communicated and when.

Do you communicate with residential borrowers?

No. Veteran Loan Servicing services commercial real estate and business/commercial loans only.
