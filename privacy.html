Privacy Policy & Institutional Data Disclosures

System Timestamp: July 2026

Veteran Mortgage Servicing, LLC, doing business as Veteran Loan Servicing ("Platform", "we", "us"), operates as an institutional, non-residential sub-servicing, debt tracking, and corporate insurance monitoring architecture. We maintain rigorous parameters to safeguard proprietary financial modeling, fund analytics, and communication protocols transmitted across our cloud networks.

1. Scope of Data Governance

This privacy directive rules exclusively over corporate entities, credit fund administrators, institutional asset holders, and authorized representatives executing intake validations via this domain. This platform under no parameters requests, logs, or tracks non-public personal information (NPPI) related to retail consumer accounts or individual consumer residential mortgages.

2. Data Collection and Information Architecture

When an authorized fund stakeholder interacts with our platform components, the following structures are logged:

  • Institutional Profiling Parameters: Entity names, corporate points of contact, and structural email routing nodes.
  • Portfolio Metrics: Self-selected parameters regarding portfolio volume metrics, aggregate Assets Under Management (AUM) echelons, and primary jurisdictional scopes.
  • Network Signatures: Cloudflare edge analytics, transmission timestamps, and standard network routing identifiers required to preserve structural infrastructure integrity.

3. Confidentiality Framework & Non-Disclosure

All portfolio information, default metrics, and operational requirements submitted via our tracking nodes are processed under enterprise confidentiality safeguards. We do not rent, distribute, sell, or monetize institutional intake metrics to outside data brokers, market analytics entities, or non-affiliated third parties. Information is utilized strictly to evaluate system compliance variables and construct customized portfolio management proposals.

4. Cloud Edge Routing & Security Layering

Our platform logic functions on advanced, secure edge architectures backed by Cloudflare token validations and static deployment configurations. Data transmissions executed via our inquiry structures run with enterprise transport layer security protocols (HTTPS) to mitigate internal or external network drag vulnerabilities.

5. Institutional Scope Exclusions

In accordance with our hardcoded system scope, our tools are engineered entirely outside consumer residential lending regulations. We do not archive or evaluate metrics governed by localized consumer lending protection frameworks, retail real estate disclosure parameters, or individual consumer banking fair-credit tracking directives.
