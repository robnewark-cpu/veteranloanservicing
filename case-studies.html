Our Approach

What Working With Us Looks Like

Veteran Loan Servicing is a growing institutional platform, currently onboarding portfolio partners. Rather than share past client details, here's exactly how an engagement runs from onboarding through ongoing servicing.

Engagement Process

From Onboarding to Ongoing Servicing

1. Portfolio Review

We start by reviewing your existing loan portfolio, current servicing setup, and reporting requirements to scope the engagement accurately.

2. Onboarding & Ledger Migration

Loan data, payment schedules, and lender disbursement details are migrated into our servicing platform with a full reconciliation check before go-live.

3. Ongoing Servicing

Once live, we handle borrower payment collection, lender disbursement, delinquency tracking, and insurance compliance monitoring on an ongoing basis.

4. Reporting & Review

You receive regular portfolio reporting, with our team available directly for questions rather than routed through a support queue.

Early Partner Program

We're Onboarding Our First Institutional Partners

As a growing platform, we're working closely with early institutional clients to fit the servicing infrastructure to their specific portfolio needs — with direct access to our founder throughout the engagement rather than a large account-management layer.
