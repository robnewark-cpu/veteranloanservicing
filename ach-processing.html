Servicing Operations

ACH processing — an active capability

ACH payment processing is an active, current operational capability. This page is the single, consistent description of how it works.

Process

Veteran Loan Servicing collects borrower payments via ACH (Automated Clearing House) and disburses to lenders on a scheduled monthly cycle. Collection and disbursement are structured around NACHA operating guidelines, and every transaction is recorded in a reconcilable audit trail. ACH payment processing is an active, current operational capability.

  • Collection — borrower payments collected via ACH on the scheduled cycle
  • Reconciliation — each transaction recorded in a reconcilable audit trail
  • Disbursement — funds disbursed to lenders on a scheduled monthly cycle
  • Reporting — disbursement activity appears in lender portfolio reporting

Controls

ACH activity is structured around NACHA operating guidelines. Each transaction carries an audit-trail entry, and collection is reconciled against the loan ledger before disbursement.

Reporting

Every ACH transaction is reconcilable and retained, supporting lender reporting and examiner or LP review.

Benefits

A single, consistent ACH workflow — the same description everywhere on this site and in our chatbot — means lenders and borrowers get one straight answer about how money moves.

FAQ

Is ACH processing active today?

Yes. ACH payment collection and lender disbursement are active, current operational capabilities.

How are borrower payments collected and disbursed to lenders?

Borrower payments are collected via ACH and disbursed to lenders on a scheduled monthly cycle, structured around NACHA operating guidelines, with a full audit trail maintained for every transaction.

Is Veteran Loan Servicing NACHA certified?

Veteran Loan Servicing structures its ACH payment processing around NACHA operating guidelines. NACHA does not certify individual companies; it publishes the operating rules that govern ACH transactions in the United States.
